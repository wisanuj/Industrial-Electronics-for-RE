Learn to read the picture first:
Yellow dots in a circuit are conventional current, leaving the positive terminal. Dot speed is proportional to current, so a diode that does not conduct has no dots running through it. In the semiconductor pictures cyan dots are electrons and pink rings are holes (places an electron is missing from). I–VThe characteristic (a diode's current against its voltage) is drawn as in the book: milliamperes and tenths of volts on the positive side, tens of volts on the negative side. The yellow dot is the circuit's operating point. 🖱Hover over a part to read it and click a switch to toggle it; every problem opens its solution one step at a time with a button.
